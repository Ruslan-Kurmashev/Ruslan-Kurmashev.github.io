Research projects

My research programme focuses on heterogeneity in autism and neurodevelopment. The projects below connect three levels of the same problem: whether computational findings generalise, how biological variation is organised, and how evidence can improve clinical pathways.

Computational robustness

Cross-dataset generalisation, domain shift, reproducible modelling and external portability across heterogeneous biomedical data.

Biological heterogeneity

Neuroimaging, transcriptomics, neurogenetics and behavioral variation across developmental and molecular levels.

Clinical translation

Early identification, referral pathways, biomarker validity and practical routes from evidence to assessment and support.

Featured projects

Three projects that best represent the current research programme and its computational, biological and translational components.

Computational robustness · 2026

AutDB: Cross-Dataset Autism Movement Benchmark

Tests whether autism-related movement models retain useful signal when transferred between datasets with different feature spaces, protocols and distributions.

Question
Does strong within-dataset performance survive real cross-dataset transfer?
Methods
Logistic regression, XGBoost, CORAL, importance weighting, PCA, leave-one-dataset-out evaluation.
Outputs
ISMB 2026 oral, ESHG 2026 poster, All-Island Computational Neuroscience lightning talk, Zenodo preprint and public code.

Biological heterogeneity · 2025

Developmental Variation in Autism Neuroanatomy

Examines whether age changes the relationship between autism symptom domains and subcortical brain volumes across a multi-site structural MRI cohort.

Question
Are symptom-neuroanatomy associations stable across age, or developmentally moderated?
Methods
ABIDE I, 355 autistic participants, 16 sites, seven bilateral subcortical regions, ADI-R domains and moderation analysis.
Output
Original research article published 30 December 2025.

Clinical translation · active

Early Autism Diagnostic Cascade

Studies measurable failure points between developmental concern, screening, referral, diagnostic assessment and access to support.

Question
Where does the early identification pathway fail after concern or a positive screen?
Design
Mixed-methods implementation research focused on escalation, referral, assessment delay and support access.
Outputs
Active pilot design, ResearchHub report, peer-reviewed evidence synthesis and conference proceedings.

Research portfolio

Additional projects are grouped by the research question they help address rather than by publication type.

Biological and behavioral heterogeneity

Work examining variation across molecular, neuroanatomical and behavioral levels in autism.

  • Cortical Transcriptomics and Molecular Convergence in Autism

    Evidence synthesis of recurrent synaptic, immune-glial and RNA-regulatory signals in the autistic cerebral cortex, with attention to brain region, development, cell type and analytical platform.

    Outputs →

  • UBE3A Dosage and Syndromic Autism Phenotypes

    A defined neurogenetic model examining how opposite directions of UBE3A dosage imbalance connect Angelman syndrome and maternally derived Dup15q-associated autism phenotypes.

    Review →

  • Behavioral Phenotyping with Q-CHAT

    Secondary analysis of an open toddler dataset examining how autism-related group differences vary across social/communication, non-social/behavioral and speech/language domains.

    Publication →

Clinical translation and implementation

Work focused on whether research evidence improves assessment, support pathways and practical decision-making.

  • RNA Biomarkers for Autism Assessment

    Critical appraisal of transcriptomic and RNA-based biomarker evidence across brain, blood and saliva, emphasizing independent validation and added clinical value beyond existing developmental assessment.

    Review →

  • Autism School

    An early-support parent programme designed to bridge the gap between developmental or diagnostic concerns and access to formal services using structured, practical and evidence-informed guidance.

    Project →
The portfolio is intentionally organized around research problems, not around a list of isolated topics. Computational robustness, biological heterogeneity and clinical translation are treated as connected parts of the same neurodevelopmental research programme.
