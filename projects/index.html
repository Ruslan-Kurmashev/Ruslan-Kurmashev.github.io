projects

Selected research and translational work. The focus here is on the scientific question, methods, current status and concrete outputs rather than on decorative project cards.

  • machine learning · autism · featured

    Transfer Learning for Autism Detection

    Cross-dataset transfer learning for autism detection when datasets have different feature spaces, distributions and population characteristics. The central question is whether models capture transferable signal rather than dataset-specific structure.

    Transfer learningDomain adaptationBiomedical AI

    Outputs: ISMB 2026 oral presentation; All-Island Computational Neuroscience Symposium lightning talk.

  • neuroimaging · ABIDE I

    Developmental Variation in Autism Neuroanatomy

    Multi-site structural MRI analysis examining whether age moderates associations between autism symptom domains and subcortical brain volumes.

    Structural MRIModeration analysisMulti-site data

    Status: research and manuscript development.

  • implementation · early assessment

    Early Autism Diagnostic Cascade

    A translational project examining a structured pathway from early concern and screening through referral, multidisciplinary assessment and support, with emphasis on measurable failure points and real-world implementation.

    Early identificationHealthcare pathwayImplementation

    Status: pilot / development.

  • transcriptomics · evidence synthesis

    Cortical Transcriptomics in Autism

    Work on convergent synaptic, immune-glial and RNA-regulatory mechanisms in the autistic cerebral cortex.

  • behavioral data · Q-CHAT

    Behavioral Phenotyping with Q-CHAT

    Secondary analysis of an open Polish toddler dataset examining how autism-related group differences vary across behavioral domains.

  • biomarkers · translational research

    RNA Biomarkers for Early Autism Assessment

    Evaluation of the clinical promise, validation gaps and translational readiness of RNA-based biomarkers for autism assessment.
