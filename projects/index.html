  • machine learning · autism · featured

    Transfer Learning for Autism Detection

    Cross-dataset transfer learning for autism-related movement data when datasets differ in feature spaces, distributions and population characteristics. The central question is whether models capture transferable signal rather than dataset-specific structure.

    Transfer learningDomain adaptationCross-dataset generalisationBiomedical AI

    Methods: logistic regression, XGBoost, CORAL, importance weighting, PCA-based representation and leave-one-dataset-out evaluation.

    Outputs: ISMB 2026 oral presentation; ESHG 2026 e-poster; Third All-Island Computational Neuroscience Symposium lightning talk; Zenodo preprint and public code.

  • neuroimaging · ABIDE I · featured

    Developmental Variation in Autism Neuroanatomy

    Multi-site structural MRI analysis examining whether age moderates associations between autism-related symptom domains and subcortical brain volumes. The project is designed to test developmental heterogeneity rather than assume one age-invariant neuroanatomical pattern.

    Structural MRIABIDE IModeration analysisMulti-site data

    Analytic sample: 355 autistic participants from 16 ABIDE I sites; seven bilateral subcortical regions; ADI-R symptom dimensions; multi-site robustness analyses.

    Status: published 30 December 2025 in Saudi Journal of Medical and Pharmaceutical Sciences.

  • implementation · early identification · featured

    Early Autism Diagnostic Cascade

    A translational project examining the pathway from developmental concern and screening through referral, multidisciplinary assessment and access to support. The goal is to identify measurable failure points rather than treat screening performance as the sole determinant of timely identification.

    Early identificationHealthcare pathwayImplementationAutism services

    Status: mixed-methods pilot design / active development. An open ResearchHub report and related peer-reviewed evidence synthesis are available.

  • social innovation · autism support · award-winning

    Autism School

    An early-support parent programme developed to bridge the gap between developmental or diagnostic concerns and access to formal services. The model focuses on structured, practical, evidence-informed guidance for families of young autistic children and families awaiting support.

    Parent supportEarly intervention pathwayDigital healthSocial enterprise

    Recognition: Best Social Enterprise Award, MTU Prize for Innovation 2026; Silver Winner, TITAN Health Awards 2026, Provider & Services — Digital Services for Children and Young People.

  • neurogenetics · dosage biology

    UBE3A Dosage and Syndromic Autism Phenotypes

    A molecular framework examining how opposite directions of UBE3A dosage imbalance connect Angelman syndrome and maternally derived Dup15q-associated autism phenotypes. The work uses UBE3A as a defined mechanistic model rather than as a general explanation for autism.
