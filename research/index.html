Research

Computational approaches to neurodevelopmental health

My research connects clinically meaningful questions in autism and neurodevelopment with computational biology, biomedical data analysis and machine learning.

01

Autism & Neurodevelopment

Behavioral heterogeneity, early assessment, developmental variation, neuroimaging, molecular mechanisms and translational pathways in autism.

02

Computational Biology & Biomedical Data

Integration and quantitative analysis of behavioral, neuroimaging and molecular datasets, with emphasis on reproducibility and robust inference.

03

AI for Healthcare

Machine learning, transfer learning, domain adaptation and evaluation of models across heterogeneous biomedical datasets and populations.

Current Questions

Questions driving the work

How does autism-related biological and behavioral heterogeneity vary across development?

Rather than treating autism as a single homogeneous signal, I am interested in age, phenotype and dataset effects that shape observed associations.

How can computational models generalise across heterogeneous datasets?

Cross-dataset generalisation, domain shift and reproducibility are central problems when translating biomedical machine learning into useful evidence.

Which neurobiological signals are sufficiently robust for clinically meaningful interpretation?

How can evidence be translated into earlier and more coherent autism assessment pathways?

Methods & Data

Methods follow the question

Methods

Statistical modelling, moderation analysis, machine learning, transfer learning, domain adaptation, dimensionality reduction and sensitivity analysis.

Data

Behavioral and questionnaire data, structural neuroimaging, transcriptomic data and clinical or observational datasets.

Tools

Python, R, SQL and reproducible data-analysis workflows.
