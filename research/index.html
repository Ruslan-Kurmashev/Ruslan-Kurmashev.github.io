research

My work focuses on computational approaches to autism and neurodevelopment, with methods chosen to answer clinically and biologically meaningful questions rather than to maximise technical complexity.

autism & neurodevelopment

I study behavioral heterogeneity, early assessment, developmental variation, neuroanatomical differences and biological mechanisms in autism. A recurring theme is that associations observed in one age group, dataset or modality should not automatically be assumed to generalise elsewhere.

computational biology & biomedical data

My work uses quantitative and computational methods to analyse behavioral, structural neuroimaging and molecular data. I am particularly interested in robust inference, reproducibility, sensitivity analysis and the interpretation of heterogeneous biomedical datasets.

AI for healthcare

I use machine learning where it contributes to the scientific or clinical question, including transfer learning, domain adaptation and cross-dataset generalisation. I am especially interested in situations where models must operate across non-identical feature spaces, sites or populations.

current questions

  • How does autism-related biological and behavioral heterogeneity vary across development?
  • Which neurobiological and molecular signals are robust enough for clinically meaningful interpretation?
  • How can machine-learning models generalise across heterogeneous biomedical datasets and populations?
  • How can research evidence be translated into earlier and more coherent autism assessment pathways?

methods & data

Methods: statistical modelling, moderation analysis, machine learning, transfer learning, domain adaptation, dimensionality reduction and sensitivity analysis.

Data: behavioral and questionnaire data, structural neuroimaging, transcriptomic data, and clinical or observational datasets.

Tools: Python, R, SQL and reproducible data-analysis workflows.
