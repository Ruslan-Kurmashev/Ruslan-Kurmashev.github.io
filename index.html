Computational Biology · Autism Research

Ruslan Kurmashev

Medical doctor · Computational biology researcher · Autism & neurodevelopment

I am a medical doctor and computational biology researcher working at the intersection of autism, neurodevelopment, biomedical data science and artificial intelligence.

My research spans behavioral phenotyping, structural neuroimaging, transcriptomics, biomarkers and machine learning. I am particularly interested in biological and clinical heterogeneity in autism, the generalizability of computational models across heterogeneous datasets, and the translation of research evidence into earlier and more reliable assessment pathways.

Alongside my own research, I contribute to scientific peer review, scholarly publishing, scientific-community development and initiatives related to autism assessment and research infrastructure.
