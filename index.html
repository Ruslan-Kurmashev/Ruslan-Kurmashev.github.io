About

Researcher in computational biology and neurodevelopmental health.

Ruslan Kurmashev is a medical doctor and computational biology researcher based in Ireland, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, neurodevelopment and biomedical data science.

His research focuses on autism, with interests spanning behavioral phenotyping, structural neuroimaging, transcriptomics, biomarkers and machine learning for heterogeneous biomedical datasets. His work aims to connect computational methods with clinically meaningful questions in early assessment, biological heterogeneity and translational healthcare research.

Beyond his own research, he contributes to scientific peer review, scholarly publishing and international research communities, and is involved in initiatives aimed at strengthening autism assessment and research infrastructure.
