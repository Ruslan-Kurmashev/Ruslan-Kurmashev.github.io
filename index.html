Computational Biology · Autism Research

Ruslan Kurmashev, MD

Computational biology researcher · Autism & neurodevelopment

I am a medical doctor and computational biology researcher studying autism and neurodevelopment across behavioral, molecular, neuroimaging and computational levels.

My work focuses on biological and clinical heterogeneity in autism, the generalisability of models across heterogeneous biomedical datasets, and the translation of research evidence into earlier and more reliable assessment and support pathways.

MSc Computational Biology candidate, Munster Technological University. Alongside my research, I contribute to scientific peer review, scholarly publishing and international research-community development.

Featured Research

  • AutDB: Cross-Dataset Autism Movement Benchmark

    Transfer learning and domain adaptation across heterogeneous autism-related movement datasets, with emphasis on external portability rather than within-dataset performance.

  • Developmental Variation in Autism Neuroanatomy

    Multi-site ABIDE I structural MRI study testing whether age moderates associations between autism symptom domains and subcortical brain volumes.

  • Early Autism Diagnostic Cascade

    Mixed-methods implementation research examining failure points between developmental concern, screening, referral, multidisciplinary assessment and access to support.
