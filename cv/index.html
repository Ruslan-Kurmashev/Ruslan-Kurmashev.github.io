Profile

My research asks how heterogeneous biological, developmental and dataset-level signals in autism can be converted into findings that remain interpretable, reproducible and clinically meaningful. I combine a medical background with computational biology and biomedical data analysis, and contribute to peer review, scientific publishing, research-community development and evidence-informed public-health work.

Research Programme

Computational robustness. Transfer learning, domain shift, external generalisation and reproducible modelling across heterogeneous biomedical datasets.

Biological heterogeneity. Transcriptomics, neurogenetics and structural neuroimaging to examine convergent and developmentally variable mechanisms.

Clinical translation. Early identification, biomarkers and implementation pathways linking research signals to assessment, referral and support.
