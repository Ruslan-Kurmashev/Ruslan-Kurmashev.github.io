profile

My work sits at the intersection of autism and neurodevelopment, computational biology and biomedical artificial intelligence. Research interests span behavioral phenotyping, structural neuroimaging, transcriptomics, early assessment and machine learning across heterogeneous biomedical datasets. I also contribute to scientific peer review, scholarly publishing and research-community development.
