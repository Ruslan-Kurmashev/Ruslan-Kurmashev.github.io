profile

Medical doctor and computational biology researcher based in Ireland. My work focuses on autism and neurodevelopment across behavioral phenotyping, structural neuroimaging, transcriptomics, RNA biomarkers, early identification and cross-dataset machine learning. Professional experience also includes production AI quality evaluation in guideline-driven workflows. Alongside research, I contribute to scientific peer review, editorial and publishing development, scientific-community leadership and evidence-informed public-health work.
