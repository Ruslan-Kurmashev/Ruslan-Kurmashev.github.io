НЕКОТОРЫЕ ОСОБЕННОСТИ В СТРОЕНИИ ТАЗОБЕДРЕННОГО СУСТАВА

С. Н. Романюк, Р. Ж. Курмашев, Н. А. Коскосиди · Основные проблемы в современной медицине, Issue II · International scientific-practical conference · Volgograd · 10 October 2015 · pp. 204–207

Proceedings PDF

Abstract

Structured summary from the published proceedings. The original paper does not contain a separately labelled abstract. The study examined anatomical measurements relevant to pre-operative planning for hip arthroplasty using 16 human skeletal specimens. Measurements included intercristal and interacetabular distances, pelvic height, femoral length, femoral-neck length, neck-shaft angle and femoral antetorsion. The published analysis reports relationships among these parameters, gives observed ranges and mean values, and emphasizes accurate implant sizing and component orientation as part of complication prevention.

Published findings

The proceedings report a mean femoral length of approximately 411 mm, mean femoral-neck length of 100 mm, mean pelvic height of 95.5 mm and a mean neck-shaft angle of 122°. The authors conclude that knowledge of common and extreme anatomical dimensions can support operative planning for hip-joint arthroplasty.
