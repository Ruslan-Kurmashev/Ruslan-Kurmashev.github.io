Salivary RNA biomarkers for autism assessment in children: clinical promise, validation gaps and practical relevance for developmental disability pathways

Ruslan Kurmashev · International Journal of Developmental Disabilities

DOI / Publisher

Overview

This review examines the clinical promise and current validation gaps of salivary RNA biomarkers for autism assessment in children, with particular attention to their practical relevance for developmental disability pathways. The article was published online on 6 September 2026 in the International Journal of Developmental Disabilities.
