Mini-review · 2017

Predictive immunological biomarkers for diagnosis and progression of latent tuberculosis: a mini-review

Russian title: Прогностические иммунологические биомаркеры диагностики и прогрессирования латентного туберкулёза (мини-обзор)

A. S. Tarabayeva, A. A. Abilbayeva, A. S. Rakisheva, Ruslan Kurmashev, K. S. Sarniyazova, A. Ya. Abubakirov · Vestnik KazNMU, No. 4, 2017, pp. 474–477

ResearchGate

Publication summary

This mini-review discusses immunological biomarkers relevant to the diagnosis of latent tuberculosis infection and to the risk of progression toward active disease. It is one of my earliest co-authored research publications and reflects my medical-school research involvement in immunology and infectious disease.
