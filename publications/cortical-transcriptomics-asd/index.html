Cortical transcriptomic dysregulation in Autism spectrum disorder: A conceptual synthesis

Ruslan Kurmashev · IBRO Neuroscience Reports · 21:63–68

DOIPubMedPMC full text

Abstract

This conceptual synthesis reviews human post-mortem cortical transcriptomic findings in autism and organises recurrent observations into three interacting domains: synaptic and neuronal programmes, immune-glial programmes and RNA-regulatory processes. It highlights the consistency of some broad molecular directions while also stressing substantial dependence on brain region, developmental stage, cellular composition and analytical method. The article positions cortical transcriptomics as a useful framework for understanding biological heterogeneity rather than as a direct diagnostic biomarker.
