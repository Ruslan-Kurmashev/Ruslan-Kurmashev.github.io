Transcriptomic Convergence in Autism Spectrum Disorder: Synaptic, Immune-Glial and RNA-Regulatory Axes in the Human Cerebral Cortex

Ruslan Kurmashev · International Journal of Developmental Neuroscience · 86(5):e70157

DOIPubMedPMC full text

Abstract

This structured review examines whether heterogeneous genetic and developmental liabilities in autism converge on shared molecular programmes in the human cerebral cortex. The synthesis focuses on recurrent synaptic and neuronal changes, immune-glial activation and RNA-regulatory abnormalities, while emphasising the importance of brain region, developmental stage, cell type and analytical platform. The review argues that cortical transcriptomic findings are most useful as a framework for biological convergence and mechanistic interpretation rather than as a stand-alone diagnostic biomarker.
