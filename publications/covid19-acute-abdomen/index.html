Systematic review · 2020

Acute Abdomen as a Nonspecific Manifestation of COVID-19: A Systematic Review (ОСТРЫЙ ЖИВОТ, КАК НЕСПЕЦИФИЧЕСКОЕ ПРОЯВЛЕНИЕ COVID-19. СИСТЕМАТИЧЕСКИЙ ОБЗОР)

Ruslan Zh. Kurmashev, Adina T. Yessimova, Akmaral G. Seysembiyeva, Umit D. Kabylbek, Gufronjon T. Rakhimov, Farukhjon R. Boynurov · Student Bulletin (Студенческий вестник) · No. 43 (141) · 2020 · pp. 48–59

Abstract

Structured summary of the published systematic review. The review examined reports of acute-abdomen presentations associated with COVID-19 during the early phase of the pandemic. The authors searched PubMed, Google Scholar and CyberLeninka and assessed included reports using a JBI critical-appraisal checklist. Twelve studies were included, comprising case reports and case series; the review highlighted patients with acute abdominal symptoms in whom abdominal pathology was not demonstrated while pulmonary findings and SARS-CoV-2 infection were identified.
