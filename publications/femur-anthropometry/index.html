Selected Anthropometric Measurements of the Femur (Некоторые антропометрические показатели бедренной кости)

S. N. Romanyuk, Ruslan Zh. Kurmashev, N. A. Koskosidi · All-Russian Youth Forum with International Participation, Science Week – 2016 (Неделя науки – 2016) · Stavropol · 17–18 November 2016 · pp. 236–239

Abstract

The study evaluated anthropometric parameters of the femur, including the diameter of the medullary canal and the external diameter of the femur at the level of the lower border of the lesser trochanter. The analysis was based on 48 radiographs and corresponding patient medical records. Mean cortical thickness was calculated, and relationships among measurements were assessed using Spearman correlation. A moderate correlation was identified (r = 0.5531), indicating that greater femoral diameter was associated with greater cortical thickness in the study group.
