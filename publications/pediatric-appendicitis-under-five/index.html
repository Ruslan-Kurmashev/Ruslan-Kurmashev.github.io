КЛИНИКА ОСТРОГО АППЕНДИЦИТА У ДЕТЕЙ ДО 5 ЛЕТ

Р. Ж. Курмашев, А. А. Курмангали · Научный руководитель: к.м.н., профессор С. Н. Ромнюк · Неделя науки – 2018 · pp. 392–393

Proceedings PDF

Summary

This conference paper reviews the clinical presentation of acute appendicitis in children younger than five years, emphasizing the diagnostic difficulty created by nonspecific symptoms, limited communication in very young children and the high risk of delayed recognition and perforation.
