КЛИНИКА ОСТРОГО АППЕНДИЦИТА У ДЕТЕЙ ДО 5 ЛЕТ

Р. Ж. Курмашев, А. А. Курмангали · Научный руководитель: к.м.н., профессор С. Н. Ромнюк · Неделя науки – 2018 · pp. 392–393

Proceedings PDF

Abstract

Structured summary from the published proceedings. The original conference paper does not provide a separately labelled abstract. It reviews the clinical presentation of acute appendicitis in children younger than five years and emphasizes the diagnostic difficulty created by nonspecific symptoms, limited ability of very young children to describe pain and overlap with other childhood illnesses. The paper highlights delayed recognition as an important contributor to perforation, abscess, peritonitis and sepsis.

Published clinical points

The proceedings describe age-dependent presentations. In infants and children younger than three, vomiting, abdominal pain, fever and diarrhea are common but nonspecific; diffuse abdominal guarding is reported more often than localized right-lower-quadrant pain. The paper notes that diagnostic delay in this age group can extend to several days and is associated with a high frequency of perforation.
