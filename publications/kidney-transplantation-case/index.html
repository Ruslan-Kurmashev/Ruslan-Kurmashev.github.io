Clinical Case of Kidney Transplantation (Клинический случай трансплантации почки)

G. M. Khaibullina, Ruslan Zh. Kurmashev, T. M. Kumarbekov, Z. K. Zakirov, A. M. Utezhanova, A. E. Bekenova, A. A. Satayeva, G. M. Karatayeva, G. A. Toyshibekova, B. B. Dauletbayeva · Bulletin of the Kazakh National Medical University (Вестник КазНМУ), No. 4, 2018, pp. 95–102

Official issue PDFResearchGate

Abstract

This article presents a clinical case demonstrating that while maintenance hemodialysis and continuous ambulatory peritoneal dialysis are methods of renal replacement therapy, kidney transplantation is a highly effective treatment for end-stage chronic renal failure and a life-saving intervention. The prospects for developing renal replacement therapy and integrating it into clinical practice depend on improving all stages of kidney transplantation, from the legislative framework to competent patient management before and after surgery.
