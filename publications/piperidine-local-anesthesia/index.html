Experimental study · 2018

Experimental study of local anesthetic activity of a row new derivatives of piperidin on the model of infiltration anesthesia

Russian title: Экспериментальное изучение местноанестезирующей активности среди новых производных пиперидина на модели инфильтрационной анестезии

D. M. Kadyrova, G. S. Smagulova, I. I. Kim, K. D. Praliev, V. K. Yu, E. M. Satbaeva, R. Zh. Kurmashev · Pharmacy of Kazakhstan, No. 5 (202), May 2018, pp. 29–31

Official issue PDF

Publication summary

This experimental pharmacology paper evaluated the local-anesthetic activity of newly synthesised piperidine derivatives using an infiltration-anesthesia model. It is an early collaborative research output from my medical and pharmacology training period.
