Failure points in the early autism identification pathway for children aged 0–5 years: Why screening is not diagnosis

Ruslan Kurmashev, Mavile Karaieva · Pediatric Investigation

DOIPubMedPMC full text

Abstract

This critical narrative review treats early autism identification as a linked clinical pathway rather than as a problem that can be solved by a single screening instrument. It examines where delay can occur between first concern, developmental surveillance, screening, referral, diagnostic assessment and access to support. The central argument is that screening is useful only when positive concerns reliably trigger escalation and timely action, and that improvement should therefore be measured across the full pathway rather than by test accuracy alone.
