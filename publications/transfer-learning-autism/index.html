Transfer learning from datasets with unshared features for detecting Autism Spectrum Disorder using AutDB

Ruslan Kurmashev, Adina Yessimova, Denis Traore, Olaitan I. Awe

Zenodo DOICode

Abstract

This work develops a reproducible benchmark for evaluating cross-dataset transfer in autism-related movement data when datasets differ in modality, protocol and feature space. Two open privacy-preserving datasets were harmonised into a common metadata structure, then evaluated using logistic regression and XGBoost under within-dataset and leave-one-dataset-out settings. The study explicitly quantifies domain shift and tests simple adaptation baselines including CORAL, importance weighting and PCA-based representation. Results show that strong within-dataset performance does not imply portability: cross-dataset performance was fragile and strongly affected by scaling, distribution shift and representation choices.
